ZEvent multiview: the 9 most-watched ZEvent 2026 streams on one screen

Multiview for ZEvent is a free, fan-made multistream page that reads the official live list from zevent.fr, keeps the streamers who are online, sorts them by viewers and plays the top 9 at once: one program feed with its Twitch chat, eight live thumbnails with viewer counts. Press 1–9 to switch program, refresh channels to re-read the list. Version française.

Updated 6 September 2026 · Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with ZEvent, ZeratoR or Twitch · Donate only on zevent.fr

How to watch several ZEvent streams at the same time

  1. Open this page during the event. The top 9 live ZEvent channels load automatically; slot 1 is the most-watched one.
  2. Press 1–9 or click a thumbnail to put that streamer on program with audio and chat.
  3. Click refresh channels whenever you want the current top 9. Your program choice is kept if that streamer is still in the list.
  4. Want your own line-up? Switch to manual mode and type any ZEvent channels, for example /zerator/antoinedaniel/mistermv.

ZEvent multiview FAQ

How can I watch several ZEvent streams at the same time?

Open this page. It fetches the official live list from zevent.fr, keeps the online streamers, sorts them by viewers and shows the top 9 at once: one program feed with its chat and eight live thumbnails. Press 1 to 9 to switch program.

Which ZEvent streamers are shown?

The 9 participating channels with the most viewers at the moment you open the page, according to zevent.fr. Click refresh channels to re-read the list; your program choice is kept if that streamer is still in the top 9. For a custom line-up, use the manual mode and type any ZEvent channel names.

Do my views still count for the streamers and for ZEvent?

Yes. Multiview uses the official Twitch embedded player for every channel, so views, ads and chat behave as on twitch.tv. Donations are made on zevent.fr only; this site never handles money.

When is ZEvent 2026?

ZEvent 2026 runs from Friday 4 September 2026 (6 pm Paris time) to Monday 7 September 2026, with an opening concert in Montpellier on 3 September. It is presented as the tenth and final edition. Check zevent.fr for the official schedule and donation total.

Is this the official ZEvent multiview?

No. Multiview is a free fan-made tool, not affiliated with ZEvent, ZeratoR or Twitch. The official site is zevent.fr and the official channel is twitch.tv/zevent.

Why only 9 streams?

Nine live Twitch players is about the most a laptop can decode at once while keeping the program feed smooth. Nine also matches a classic 3×3 multiviewer and the number keys on a keyboard.
